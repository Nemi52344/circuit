# Built to carry — campaign copy

One idea across three formats: the Challenger carries a load, and the battery is not the thing
to be afraid of. Every figure below appears on bncmotors.in. Nothing is rounded up, inferred or
softened, and no price, range, top speed or delivery date appears anywhere — those move, and a
caption that outlives the fact it quotes is a liability.

## What is deliberately not here

- **No price.** ₹99,900 is on the site but carries "subject to change prior to delivery". A
  poster outlives that caveat.
- **No range or top speed.** The Challenger page shows unlabelled figures (90, 65, 30, 14) and
  the Boss page shows others. Without knowing which is range, which is speed and under what
  conditions, any caption using them is a guess with a number in it.
- **No subsidy or EMI claim.** Those change by state and by month.

Anything above can be added once somebody confirms it against the spec sheet.
